For days an alert told me I had not submitted something I had already submitted.

It fired every six hours. The only way to clear it was a command typed on my laptop. The alert arrives on my phone.

So there was no moment where I could both see it and act on it.

What it claimed to track was unknowable anyway. The platform it asked about has no API, so nothing could ever confirm the submission.

It asks once now.
