A deceptively simple guessing game wired straight into the places people already scroll. You pick a number, the game bites back, and your hits and misses become something worth sharing — the loop is built for the feed, not a landing page nobody visits.

Under the hood it ties a live web app to social distribution, so a round played is a round posted. Named players opt in; the game does the rest. Shipped, live, and still getting sharper.
